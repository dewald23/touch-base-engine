## Part 5: Response Interpretation & Action Playbook

### **Scenario A: "Thanks, but we already have someone handling our website."**

**Interpretation:** Polite deflection. Standard business defense.

**Your Action:**
1. Do not argue or pitch harder
2. Reply: `"Completely understand. Keep the preview link bookmarked—if you ever want a second opinion on your mobile lead capture or map pack rank, my door is open. No pressure."`
3. Update CRM:
   - Status → **8 - Passed**
   - Col L (Response) → "Not Interested"
   - Notes → "Already has agency. Keep warm for 60 days."

**Next Step:** Move to nurture list. Do not contact again for 2 months.

---

### **Scenario B: "How much does something like this cost?"**

**Interpretation:** Warm lead. Actively evaluating value. Price-conscious but interested.

**Your Action:**
1. Do NOT send a flat fee over WhatsApp
2. Reply: `"Investment depends on whether you need a speed/conversion refresh or a full lead-capture funnel. Let's do a quick 5-minute call so I can show you what we've fixed for other Overberg businesses. When works—Tuesday or Wednesday?"`
3. Update CRM:
   - Status → **4 - Warm / Reply**
   - Col L (Response) → "Interested"
   - Notes → "Asking about pricing. Schedule discovery call."
4. Send calendar link if they agree to call

**Next Step:** Book discovery call (Days 8–10). Run Agent 4 proposal before the call.

---

### **Scenario C: "Can you call me on [Day] at [Time]?"**

**Interpretation:** Hot lead. Ready to move forward. Highest priority.

**Your Action:**
1. Confirm immediately: `"Perfect. I'll call you at [Time] on [Day]. Looking forward to it."`
2. Prepare before the call:
   - Run Agent 4 to generate your closing proposal brief
   - Review their SEO audit (Col G) and pain points
   - Prepare 2–3 specific questions based on their business
3. Update CRM:
   - Status → **5 - Discovery Call**
   - Col L → "Call Scheduled"
   - Notes → "Discovery call [date/time]. Proposal brief ready."

**During call (15 min):**
- Ask about current lead flow
- Ask about budget range (R2–5k? R5–10k? R10k+?)
- Ask about timeline to start
- Describe your solution + expected ROI

**After call:**
- Send proposal within 2 hours
- Follow up on Day 3 after send

**Next Step:** Move to **6 - Proposal Sent** after proposal delivery.

---

### **Scenario D: "I need to discuss with my partner/accountant."**

**Interpretation:** Warm lead. Real interest, but requires internal approval.

**Your Action:**
1. Reply: `"Perfect. Here's a 1-page summary you can forward to them: [send simple PDF]. Happy to jump on a 20-minute three-way call if that helps clarify anything. Let me know."`
2. Update CRM:
   - Status → **4 - Warm / Reply** (not yet qualified)
   - Col L → "Needs Approval"
   - Notes → "Decision-maker pending. Follow up in 5 days."

**Next Step:** Follow up in 5 business days. If still pending, offer to join a group call.

---

### **Scenario E: Zero Response After 2 Follows-Ups**

**Interpretation:** Not ready, not interested, or timing is off.

**Your Action:**
1. Do NOT send third follow-up
2. Update CRM:
   - Status → **1 - Cold** (return to cold pool)
   - Col L → "No Response"
   - Notes → "Followed up x2. Not ready. Re-engage in 30 days."

**Next Step:** Add to monthly nurture email list. Re-contact in 30 days with different angle or seasonal offer.
