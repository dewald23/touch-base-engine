1. Open your Vercel dashboard → Touch Base Agency OS
2. For each of the first 5 businesses, fill in:

## Response Scenarios & Next Actions

### Scenario 1: "Positive Immediate Response"
**Response:** "This looks great! Tell me more."

**Next Action (within 4 hours):**
1. Send calendar link for 15-min discovery call
2. Prepare discovery questions (see below)
3. Have proposal template ready

### Scenario 2: "Curious but Not Sold"
**Response:** "Interesting, but I'm not sure this fits us right now."

**Next Action (within 24 hours):**
1. Send 1-minute video walkthrough of preview page
2. Ask: "What would make this valuable for you?"
3. Offer to extend timeline or customize offer

### Scenario 3: "No Response After Day 3"
**Response:** Silence after initial + Day-3 follow-up

**Next Action (Day 21):**
1. Send one final, low-pressure touch: "No pressure, just wanted to check if you had a chance to review."
2. If still no response, mark as "Not Ready" and move to nurture list
3. Add to monthly newsletter/case study email list

### Scenario 4: "Objection: Too Expensive"
**Response:** "This sounds great but is outside our budget."

**Next Action (within 24 hours):**
1. Offer a lighter version (e.g., "Local SEO Audit Only" for R1,500)
2. Ask: "What budget would feel comfortable?"
3. Create a 3-month pilot or payment plan option

### Scenario 5: "Objection: Needs to Check with Boss / Partner"
**Response:** "I need to discuss with my business partner."

**Next Action (within 48 hours):**
1. Send a 1-pager summary they can forward
2. Offer to join a 20-minute three-way call
3. Follow up in 5 business days

## Discovery Call Framework (15 minutes)

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for taking the time. The goal here is simple: I want to understand what you're trying to achieve and whether I can help. Sound good?"

### Problem Validation (5 min)
- "How are you currently generating leads?"
- "What's your biggest challenge with mobile visibility?"
- "How important is direct booking/enquiry capture for you?"
- "Are you losing business to competitors with better online presence?"

### Opportunity Sizing (4 min)
- "If we could get you 3 qualified inquiries per week, what would that be worth?"
- "What's your conversion rate from inquiry to booking?"
- "What's your average order value / project size?"

### Timeline & Commitment (3 min)
- "If this makes sense, when would you want to start?"
- "Who else needs to be involved in the decision?"
- "What would success look like 90 days from now?"

### Close (1 min)
- "Here's what I'm hearing: [summarize]. Let me put together a simple proposal and send it over. Sound good?"

- Scale the winning vertical to 30–50 businesses
- Refine messaging based on feedback

- Plan month 2 expansion to adjacent niches or geographies

**If you don't hit targets:**
- Analyze what didn't work (messaging, targeting, timing)
- Adjust scripts and preview pages
- Try a different vertical or geography
- Consider running a smaller batch (5–10) with heavily refined messaging
- Do not abandon—iterate quickly
